OUR MISSION

One heritage. A world of football.

Bangladesh Diaspora Ballers is an independent talent-identification initiative focused on discovering, documenting and promoting footballers with Bangladeshi heritage worldwide. We aim to create a bridge between players, families, clubs, academies, scouts and the wider football community.
